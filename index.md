# Mahadev Manan Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Mahadev Manan Publisher is operated by the owner of the Mahadev Manan social accounts. It creates and publishes devotional videos to accounts explicitly authorized by their owner. It does not offer public sign-up.

## Information used

The tool uses authorized account and Page identifiers, access tokens, publishing permissions, video files, captions, publication status and post identifiers. The current publishing implementation does not retrieve follower lists, private messages or audience members’ profiles. Platform providers may collect additional information under their own policies.

## Purpose and service providers

Information is used to create videos, publish to authorized accounts, confirm publication, prevent duplicate posts, recover failed operations and send operational failure alerts. Google Cloud processes content-generation requests. GitHub Actions runs the automation and stores encrypted publishing credentials and private job metadata. YouTube and Meta receive submitted videos and metadata. The configured email provider processes operational alerts. Stock media comes from licensed sources. This policy does not govern these providers’ own services.

## Storage and retention

Publishing credentials are kept in local ignored configuration and encrypted GitHub Actions secrets, rather than public source code. Production video files are temporary on hosted runners. Published videos remain on destination platforms until removed there. Small publication and recovery records are retained privately to prevent duplicates; no automatic retention deadline is currently set. Local test media and diagnostic records may remain until the operator removes them.

## Control and deletion

The account owner can stop scheduled publishing, revoke platform permissions, remove stored credentials and delete local or private repository records. Removing local records does not remove videos already published on a platform. Requests about this tool or its records can be made through the [Mahadev Manan Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61595835447682). Identity or account ownership may need to be confirmed before fulfilling a request.

## Changes

The operator will update this policy when the tool’s data practices change.
