# Mahadev Manan Publisher — Terms of Use

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Mahadev Manan Publisher is a personal tool operated by the owner of the Mahadev Manan social accounts. It prepares Hindi devotional videos and publishes content only to accounts whose owner has authorized the relevant platform access. It does not provide public registration or paid subscriptions.

## Authorized use

Only the account owner or an operator authorized by that owner may use this tool. An operator must have the rights and permissions needed for every video, image, narration, music track and caption submitted to a platform. Platform terms and publishing rules apply to all uploads.

## Account access and publication

Connecting an account permits only the access approved in the platform's authorization process. The owner can revoke that access through the destination platform and stop the publishing schedule. Publishing may be delayed, rejected or restricted by a platform, and the tool does not promise views, followers, income or uninterrupted service.

## Content and data

The operator is responsible for reviewing content, accurately disclosing synthetic media where applicable and maintaining required license records. Published videos remain on their destination platforms until removed there. Data handling and deletion controls are described in the [Privacy Policy](./).
